Before you ask.

Who lives at Ember House?

Mostly people aged 22 to 32 working in tech, consulting, design and startups. A few are doing postgraduate study. Everyone has a private or shared room and the common spaces are for all.

What's the minimum stay?

One month. Month-to-month members give 30 days' notice. The 6-month plan saves 8% and has a 3-month lock-in.

Can I have guests over?

Yes. Guests are welcome in common areas until 11 pm and can stay over in your room twice a month if your roommate (if you have one) is fine with it.

Do you give rent receipts for HRA?

Yes, every month, with the house's registered address and a PAN for the landlord.

Is food included every day?

Dinner is cooked Monday to Friday. The kitchen and pantry are open all week for everything else.
